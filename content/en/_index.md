## About Us

Swarnim Prayash Foundation is a registered, non-profit social organisation dedicated to the holistic development of underprivileged, vulnerable, and disadvantaged communities.

Our aim is not simply to provide assistance, but to help people become self-reliant by creating access to education, skills, opportunities, and the confidence to build better lives.

We work across a range of areas, including education, women’s empowerment, skill development, health awareness, environmental protection, sanitation, child welfare, youth development, and community development.

We believe that an inclusive, empowered, and developed society can only be built when every individual has access to equal opportunities.

Swarnim Prayash Foundation is guided by the values of transparency, accountability, service, and humanity. We design our initiatives around the genuine needs of the communities we serve, with a focus on creating meaningful and lasting impact.

We work alongside local communities, volunteers, donors, corporate organisations, and government bodies to bring about positive and sustainable change.

## Recent Initiative

### Cloth Donation Drive

Swarnim Prayash Foundation recently organised a cloth donation drive to collect usable clothes from people who wanted to support families facing economic hardship.

Donors were provided with certificates of appreciation to recognise their contribution and encourage continued participation in community service.

The collected clothes were then provided to children from economically disadvantaged families.

The initiative brought together donors, volunteers, and community members to help provide essential clothing to children who needed support.
